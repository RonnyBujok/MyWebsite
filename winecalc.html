Information:

  • Natural sugar is derived from the original Gravity using the ASBC Plato equation.
  • Sugar concentration = Total sugar / Total volume
  • Total volume = Juice + Water + 0.625 × Sugar (kg)
  • Estimated ABV = (OG − FG) × 131.25
  • Target OG = FG + (Target ABV / 131.25)
  • Required sugar = Target sugar concentration × Total volume
  • Sanitizing equipment:
    • Campden: 1 g/L + acid
  • Stabilization:
    • Campden: 0.1 g/L
    • Potassium sorbate: 0.5–0.75 g/L (for backsweetening)
  • Sugar → alcohol:
    • 17 g/L sugar ≈ 1% ABV
  • Sparkling sugar:
    • Light fizz: 4 g/L
    • Sparkling: 6–8 g/L
    • Champagne style: 8–10 g/L
  • Yeast nutrient:
    • 0.2–0.4 g/L
